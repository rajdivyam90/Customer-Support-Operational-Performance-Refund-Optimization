# Customer Support Operational Performance \& Refund Optimization





## Objective



The primary objective of this project is to analyze customer support ticket operations to pinpoint the primary drivers for post-sale tickets generation and how the company handles these grievances. The project focuses on finding out the core reasons why customers are unsatisfied with the sale of products and what we can do to minimise refunds and customer friction. It aims to provide actionable insights to help operations leaders prioritize high defect products categories and streamline ticket routing.





### Business Problem



Customer support operations are experiencing high ticket volume across multiple communication channels, leading to delayed resolution and increased customer friction. As a result millions in revenue are being lost to product refund causing challenges to the company.

#### 

#### Key Goals:



* Determine primary drivers for customer grievances.
* Optimize support operations.
* Measure financial Impact on revenue due to refunds
* Contrast high refund product lines against low refund items.
* Determine which channel have more traffic for tickets generation.



#### Key areas of Analysis:



* Customer behaviour analysis
* Root cause categorization
* Channel intake and volume
* Categorized refund analysis





#### Tools used



* Microsoft SQL Server – Data extraction and analysis
* Microsoft Excel – Initial data cleaning, exploration and visualizations
* Microsoft PowerPoint – Business presentation and reporting

#### 

#### Key Insights \& Recommendations



* **Insight:** Refund payouts are heavily skewed towards high ticket appliances and the ones that are usually high priced and heavy. The top 5 refund products (Air Purifier, Room heaters, Ceiling fans, water heaters and tower coolers) each account for roughly 1.6M to 1.99M in lost revenue, while bottom tier products (Epilators, Irons and steamers) lose under 265K.
* **Recommendation:** Implement  stricter pre-shipment quality checks and assurance protocols along with heavy duty packaging and careful shipment. Such products should be marked as fragile.



* **Insight:** Over 50% of total ticket volume got received within the first 4 days post purchase which shows heavy friction during initial delivery, unboxing and setup.
* **Recommendation:** Include clear instructions, QR codes and quick start guides for appliance setup and provide installation assistance from a third party vendor.



* **Insight:** About 40% tickets represent "Product Defect" \& "Damaged in transit" as the single largest driver for grievances whereas rest issues represent less than 15% each.
* **Recommendation:** Keep a closer eye on product suppliers and delivery agents, ranking them based on the number of tickets they add. Consider changing such suppliers and making stricter checks for packaging and delivery.



* **Insight:** Online/Digital platforms for grievances handle over 90% of the volume.
* **Recommendation:** Invest in automated chat bots/Live AI agents for standard tracking and delivery updates to deflect low complexity tickets from human agents so that they focus on priority tickets.



* **Insight:** Majority of the tickets come from electronic kitchen and home appliances.
* **Recommendation:** Check their suppliers and inspect their packaging condition before they leave the warehouse for delivery.



* **Insight:** Average response time decreases with the severity of the complaint. It clearly shows agents are prioritizing critical complaints first which is a good practice.
* **Recommendation:** Recruit more agents if possible to lower the average response time even further which will reduce agent load and will eventually increase agent productivity.
